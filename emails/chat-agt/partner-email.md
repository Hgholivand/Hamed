## Email

Hi {{FirstName}},

We just launched something new on our website and I'd really like your opinion on it.

It's called Chat AGT, an AI assistant that's connected to all of our product datasheets and knows the full range of solutions we offer. Instead of digging through specs to find the right product, you just tell it what you need in plain language and it recommends what fits.

If you want to talk to someone, it can connect you with a real person on our sales team. If you'd rather look through the products on your own, it'll help you find what you need right on the website.

My favourite part is that you can attach a photo. A drawing, a rough sketch, even a screenshot of the application. Chat AGT will review it and help you figure out the right solution.

This is where we need your help. You know these products and the applications they go into better than almost anyone, so we'd love for you and the team at {{CompanyName}} to give it a real test.

A few ideas to get you started:

- Ask about a product you know well and check the specs it gives you
- Describe a real job the way a customer would describe it to you
- Upload a sketch or screenshot and see what it comes back with
- Try a tricky question a customer asked you recently

Leaving feedback is quick. You'll see a thumbs up and a thumbs down with each answer. If it got it right, give it a thumbs up. If something's wrong, click thumbs down and a window will pop up where you can tell us what was wrong and why. Those notes are the most valuable part for us, because they show us exactly what to fix.

You'll find Chat AGT on our website here: [website link]

Thanks for taking a few minutes on this, {{FirstName}}. And if you have thoughts that don't fit in the pop-up, just hit reply and send them my way.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
[Title]
[Company Name]
[Phone]
